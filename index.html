⚙️ Mechanical Systems Operations Guide

Comprehensive guide for HVAC, pumps, chillers, boilers, and ventilation systems

Mechanical Systems Operations Overview

Primary Responsibilities

  • Operate and monitor all mechanical systems daily
  • Ensure equipment runs efficiently and safely
  • Perform routine inspections of machinery
  • Maintain mechanical rooms and equipment areas
  • Document operations and maintenance
  • Respond to alarms and emergencies

HVAC Systems

Air Handling Units

  • Check fan operation and belt tension
  • Monitor air temperatures
  • Inspect and replace filters
  • Verify damper operation
  • Monitor motor amperage

Pumps & Chillers

Pump Systems

  • Monitor suction/discharge pressures
  • Check for leaks around seals
  • Listen for unusual noises
  • Check bearing temperatures
  • Monitor motor current

Chiller Operations

  • Monitor refrigerant pressures
  • Check oil levels and pressure
  • Verify water flow
  • Check for refrigerant leaks
  • Monitor efficiency

Boiler Systems

Boiler Operations

  • Monitor pressure and temperature
  • Check water level in gauge glass
  • Verify burner flame
  • Test low water cutoff daily
  • Inspect safety relief valves
  • Monitor water treatment
⚠️ SAFETY CRITICAL: Never operate boiler without proper water level. Test safety devices daily.

Ventilation Systems

Exhaust and Supply Fans

  • Verify fan rotation
  • Check belt tension
  • Monitor motor temperatures
  • Inspect ductwork for leaks
  • Verify damper positions
  • Monitor static pressure

Routine Inspections

Daily Tasks

  • Walk mechanical rooms, check for leaks
  • Listen for unusual noises
  • Verify equipment running properly
  • Review and clear alarms
  • Record meter readings

Weekly Tasks

  • Inspect belts for wear
  • Check oil levels
  • Test emergency shutdown
  • Inspect electrical connections

Troubleshooting Guide

Low pressureCheck cavitation, suction pressure, impeller

Chiller Issues

High head pressureCheck condenser flow, clean tubes, verify tower
Low suctionCheck refrigerant charge, flow, leaks, valve

Boiler Issues

Won't igniteCheck fuel supply, ignition, flame scanner

🚨 Emergency Shutdown Procedures

⚠️ CRITICAL: In any emergency, prioritize human safety. Evacuate if necessary. Call 911 for fires, injuries, or hazardous conditions.

HVAC Emergency Shutdown

  1. Press emergency stop button (red)
  2. Shut off power at main disconnect
  3. Close isolation dampers
  4. Tag "DO NOT OPERATE" with lockout
  5. Document and notify supervisor
When: Smoke, fire, burning smell, severe vibration, motor overheating, electrical arcing

Pump Emergency Shutdown

  1. Press emergency stop or turn off starter
  2. Close suction/discharge valves
  3. Lockout electrical disconnect
  4. If major leak: shut water supply, contain spill
  5. Document and report

Chiller Emergency Shutdown

  1. Activate emergency stop on control panel
  2. Shut off main power disconnect
  3. Close chilled/condenser water valves
  4. If refrigerant leak: evacuate area, ventilate
  5. Contact refrigeration technician
REFRIGERANT LEAK: Evacuate immediately, activate exhaust fans if safe, post warnings, call HVAC emergency service

Boiler Emergency Shutdown

  1. IMMEDIATELY press emergency stop
  2. Shut off fuel supply (gas valve/oil pump)
  3. Shut off combustion air fan
  4. If safe, close steam isolation valves
  5. Allow to cool - DO NOT add cold water
LOW WATER: DO NOT add water, shut down immediately, cool naturally
OVERPRESSURE: Verify safety valves, reduce firing, emergency shutdown if continuing

Maintenance Checklist

  • Daily: Check basin water level and makeup operation
  • Daily: Inspect for unusual vibration or noise
  • Weekly: Test water chemistry (pH, conductivity, biocide)
  • Weekly: Inspect fill media for fouling or damage
  • Monthly: Clean strainers and check drift eliminators
  • Monthly: Inspect fan blades and gearbox oil level
  • Quarterly: Check fan belt tension and alignment
  • Quarterly: Clean basin and remove sediment
  • Semi-Annual: Inspect and clean fill media thoroughly
  • Annual: Complete tower shutdown and deep cleaning
  • Annual: Legionella testing per facility protocol
⚠️ Water Quality Limits:
• pH: 7.5-8.5 (adjust if outside range)
• Conductivity: < 1500 μS (increase blowdown if exceeded)
• Free Chlorine: 0.5-1.0 ppm
• Calcium Hardness: < 800 ppm
• If limits exceeded, contact water treatment vendor immediately

VFD Maintenance Tasks

  • Monthly: Check for error codes and fault history
  • Monthly: Verify cooling fan operation
  • Monthly: Check for unusual noise or vibration
  • Quarterly: Clean air intake filters
  • Quarterly: Check all connection tightness
  • Quarterly: Verify parameter settings unchanged
  • Semi-Annual: Measure input/output voltages and currents
  • Annual: Complete inspection and cleaning of internals
  • Annual: Test emergency stop and bypass functions
  • Annual: Update firmware if available
⚠️ VFD Safety Precautions:
• Always disconnect power and wait 5 minutes before servicing (capacitor discharge)
• Use only qualified electricians for VFD work
• Never bypass VFD protection features
• Monitor drive temperature - should be < 40°C ambient
• Keep drives clean and free of dust/debris
• Document all parameter changes in equipment log
📝 Common VFD Faults & Solutions:
• Overcurrent: Check motor load, verify proper acceleration time
• Overvoltage: Check deceleration time, verify supply voltage
• Overtemperature: Clean filters, verify cooling fan, check ambient temp
• Ground Fault: Inspect motor and cable insulation
• Communication Loss: Check BMS connections and settings

Safety Procedures

⚠️ SAFETY FIRST: Always follow lockout/tagout procedures. Never bypass safety devices.

Personal Protective Equipment

  • Safety glasses required in mechanical rooms
  • Hearing protection in high-noise areas
  • Gloves when handling equipment/chemicals
  • Steel-toed boots required
  • Hard hat in designated areas

Electrical Safety

  • De-energize equipment before maintenance
  • Use proper lockout/tagout procedures
  • Verify zero energy state before work
  • Maintain clearances around panels
  • Report damaged cords immediately
